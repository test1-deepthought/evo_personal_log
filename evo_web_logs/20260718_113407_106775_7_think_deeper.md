=== SYNTHESIS ===
The question "What is the meaning of life?" resists a single propositional
answer because it is a second-order question — it asks for the framework
within which first-order answers are evaluated. Each tradition supplies
such a framework, and the frameworks are not reducible to one another.

The meaning of life is best understood as a dialectical relationship
between objective value (the goods, virtues, and relationships that
are genuinely worthy of pursuit) and subjective meaning-making
(the authentic choices through which individuals engage with those goods).
No single formulation captures it; the search itself is partly constitutive of the answer.
